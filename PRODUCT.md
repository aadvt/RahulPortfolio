# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are prospective clients, creative collaborators, and recruiters evaluating a portfolio for fit.

## Product Purpose

Showcase Rahul's visual direction and interaction craft to drive project inquiries and collaborations.

## Brand Personality

Experimental, editorial, streetwear-forward.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS polish, corporate minimalism, and template-like portfolio layouts.

## Design Principles

- Let typography carry the attitude.
- Use motion to guide, not distract.
- Keep layouts bold but structured.
- Showcase craft through layered interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA where possible and respect reduced-motion preferences.
